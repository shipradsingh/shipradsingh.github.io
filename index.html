Cross-Lingual Transfer for Low-Resource NLP

Shipra Singh, Co-author A, Co-author B  ·  arXiv 2026 (preprint)

We study how large multilingual language models transfer knowledge to unseen languages, and propose a lightweight adapter-based fine-tuning strategy that improves accuracy by up to 12 percentage points on benchmark datasets.

NLP Cross-lingual Low-resource Preprint

Efficient Knowledge Graph Embeddings for Question Answering

Shipra Singh, Co-author C  ·  ACL 2026

We introduce a memory-efficient embedding method for large-scale knowledge graphs and demonstrate strong performance on multi-hop question answering with significantly reduced parameter counts.
