The Lab

I am a member of the XYZ Lab at University XYZ, directed by Prof. XYZ. Our lab is part of the Department of Computer Science and affiliated with the Center for Language and Intelligence.

We work at the frontier of natural language understanding, knowledge representation, and multimodal learning. We are passionate about building AI systems that are robust, interpretable, and useful in real-world, low-resource settings.

Lab Website Lab GitHub

Research Areas

🌍 Cross-lingual & Multilingual NLP

Developing methods that enable language models to generalise across hundreds of languages, including extremely low-resource ones. We study zero-shot and few-shot transfer, language-agnostic representations, and massively multilingual pretraining.

Multilingual Transfer Learning Zero-shot

🧠 Knowledge Graphs & Reasoning

Building efficient, scalable knowledge graph embeddings and integrating structured world knowledge into neural models for question answering, relation extraction, and common-sense reasoning.

Knowledge Graphs QA Reasoning

💬 Robust & Interpretable Dialogue

Studying the brittleness of task-oriented dialogue systems under distribution shift, and developing explanation methods to make dialogue model decisions transparent and trustworthy.

Dialogue Robustness Interpretability
